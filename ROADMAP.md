# Micro Cockpit roadmap

`PRODUCT.md` is the product source of truth. `CONTEXT.md` defines the domain language. This file is the only active roadmap; archived plans are historical context, not current requirements.

## Now

- Complete the owner-authored Expectation self-review flow with retained Observation, evidence, decision, and optional Trade context.
- Make Review lead with Needs Review, Recently Reviewed, evidence-backed Patterns, and Discipline Principles.
- Let a User explicitly confirm a recurring Reasoning Issue or Reasoning Strength as a meaningful Pattern.
- Let a confirmed Pattern become an optional Discipline Principle, then surface active principles quietly during future observations and decisions.
- Keep Today capture-first and improve Calendar, Watchlist, and Review links around the same decision loop.

## Next

- Deepen Pattern detail with traceable review evidence, useful dates, excerpts, and real trend data where the retained records support it.
- Improve desktop information density and narrow-phone progressive disclosure without turning the authenticated product into a dashboard.
- Strengthen completed-session Daily Close evidence and supported-market calendar behavior without adding live quotes.
- Continue feature-local frontend extraction only where it reduces caller knowledge or duplicated behavior.

## Later

- Expand evidence comparison between Human and explicitly named Agent User records while preserving separate ownership.
- Add further completed-session market evidence when a stable provider contract and clear reflection use case exist.
- Improve export and long-horizon reflection views as the volume of reviewed Expectations warrants them.

## Explicitly Not Planned

- Holdings, positions, cost basis, tax lots, portfolio allocation, or authoritative portfolio profit and loss.
- Brokerage or broker synchronization, order routing, trading signals, or automatic investment recommendations.
- Real-time quotes, intraday monitoring, or a live market terminal.
- Trade-first journaling, full `invest-diary` feature parity, ETF directory parity, blog migration, generic news, or social feeds.
- Gamification, streaks, scoring, system-authored diagnoses, or AI acting as the product's official judge.
